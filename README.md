## ✨ Features

1. **Sacred Opening Invocation**: Divine Vakratunda Mahakaya shloka with Sanskrit, English transliteration, and Shiva-Parvati hand blessing imagery.
2. **Interactive Scratch-to-Reveal Invitation**: Scratchable gold foil with cursor/touch physics, percentage progress indicator, and celebration confetti burst.
3. **Cinematic Hero & Live Countdown**: Precise timezone-aware countdown timer to November 22, 2026.
4. **4 Event Itinerary Cards**: High-resolution illustrated invitation card previews, timings, dress codes, rituals, and 1-click Google Calendar & iCal integration.
5. **Bride & Groom Lineage**: Profiles honoring both families (Rudra Family & Yanagandhula Family).
6. **Celebration Venues & Navigation**: Google Maps directions for Suryapet, Thungathurthi, and Surat with address-copying.
7. **Digital Card Gallery & Lightbox**: Full-screen lightbox viewer for all 7 authentic invitation cards with zoom, swipe, and download functionality.
8. **Interactive RSVP Form**: Guest confirmation, headcount, dietary choices (Pure Veg South Indian bhojanam), and local storage persistence.
9. **Guestbook & Blessings Wall**: Community blessings wall with pre-filled Telugu & English blessings and like reactions.
10. **Auspicious Audio Player**: Ambient Indian Tanpura meditative drone with floating vinyl player and mute controls.
11. **Ambient Particle System**: Soft drifting rose/jasmine petals and shimmering gold dust.
12. **Complete SEO & Social Sharing**: Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data, OpenGraph tags, WhatsApp 1-click sharing.
